## Sequence Prometheus registration after Istio

The commit on `main` is titled as an `iac` fix that orders the Prometheus registration after Istio. That framing tells me the intent was dependency sequencing: the Prometheus registration is meant to happen once Istio is already in place, instead of racing ahead of it. I am keeping this scoped to ordering, because that is all the commit subject establishes.
