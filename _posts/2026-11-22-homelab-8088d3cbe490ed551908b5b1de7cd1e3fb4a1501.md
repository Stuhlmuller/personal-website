This push adds a dedicated test that verifies the private hosted Renovate registry relay is excluded from the active Traefik overlay and that its deployment meets zero‑replica, service‑account, and image‑signature constraints, while the documentation now spells out the activation steps that must verify Tailnet Lock enrollment, persistent relay state, companion workflow, and OCI access before the route can be used for hosted Renovate runs.

## Introduce private hosted Renovate registry relay test

I created `scripts/ci/renovate-registry-relay-test.py` to assert that the hosted Renovate registry candidate lives outside the active Traefik resources, that its deployment runs with zero replicas, that the pod does not automount the service account token, that its service account name is `renovate-registry-relay`, that its container images are signed Harbor images with a SHA‑256 digest, and that the ping and tailscale arguments match the expected health and networking configuration. The test is imported into `scripts/ci/offline-checks.sh` so it runs as part of the standard offline validation pipeline.

## Document activation requirements in harbor-oci.md

I expanded `openwiki/operations/harbor-oci.md` to describe a separate activation process that must verify signed Tailnet Lock enrollment, persistent relay state, the protected companion workflow, and a read‑only OCI lookup plus access denials before the private TCP 443 route may be used for hosted Renovate runs. The added text makes clear that the route is a zero‑replica Kustomization outside the active Traefik Application and that each activation step provides concrete evidence of security and readiness.

## Validate network boundary and deployment constraints in CI

By importing the new test into the offline checks, the CI pipeline now automatically fails if the hosted Renovate registry candidate is mistakenly included in active resources or if its deployment does not meet the zero‑replica, service‑account, or image‑signature requirements. This prevents accidental activation of a mis‑configured relay and ensures that any subsequent manual activation gate sees a properly isolated and verified deployment.
